We model the world as it is integrating complex systems such as markets, science, geopolitics, and more.

Most big money decisions, from which startup to back to which risk to hedge, still run on spreadsheets, a few headline numbers and gut feeling. That squeezes a rich, connected world into a handful of metrics. It looks at one company at a time, mostly at its past, and misses the knock-on effects that often decide the outcome.

We combine models with live data to see the whole system: what depends on what, what happens when something breaks, and what would have happened otherwise. Less guesswork, more evidence, with simulations and what-ifs you can test before you commit.

    Why this is hard to copy
    • Several models on one map. Networks show what's connected, learning curves show when things get cheap, agents show how people react, and LLM readers keep the data fresh.

    • Scored against reality. Every forecast is checked against what actually happened.
